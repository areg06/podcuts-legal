Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Overview

Podcuts helps users turn authorized long-form videos into short clips and publish selected clips to connected social-media accounts. This policy explains the information used to provide those features.

Information we process

  • YouTube links, video metadata, subtitles, generated clips, captions, and publishing selections that you provide.
  • Account identifiers and authorization tokens supplied by YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Meta, or other connected services.
  • Basic technical logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the application.

How information is used

Information is used to download permitted source material, generate and rank clips, display previews, authenticate connected accounts, and publish content only when the user requests it.

Sharing

Podcuts sends information to connected platforms only as needed to authenticate an account or perform a requested upload. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited information when necessary to host or operate the application.

Storage and security

The current Podcuts application primarily stores projects and credentials on the operator's device. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Public media links created for platform publishing may be accessible to anyone who has the link while they remain active.

Retention and deletion

Project files and credentials are kept only as long as needed to operate the service or until the operator deletes them. Users may revoke Podcuts access through the security or connected-app settings of the relevant social platform.

Your choices

You may stop using Podcuts, revoke platform authorization, or request deletion of information associated with the service by contacting us.

Children

Podcuts is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to pocuts26@gmail.com.
